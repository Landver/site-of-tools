Certificate decoder and chain checker

Paste a certificate, a chain or a CSR as PEM, or the DER as base64 or hex. Several certificates are checked as a chain: each one against the next, and if the order is wrong, the right order. Add a key to check it belongs to the certificate.

The same with openssl

openssl x509 -in cert.pem -noout -text                  # read a certificate
openssl req -in csr.pem -noout -text                    # read a CSR
openssl x509 -in cert.pem -noout -fingerprint -sha256   # fingerprint over the DER
openssl x509 -inform DER -in cert.der -out cert.pem     # DER to PEM
openssl verify -CAfile root.pem -untrusted intermediate.pem cert.pem   # check a chain
openssl x509 -in cert.pem -noout -pubkey | openssl sha256   # key match: these two
openssl pkey -in key.pem -pubout | openssl sha256           # lines print the same
