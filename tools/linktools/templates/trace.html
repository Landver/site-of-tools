What this cannot do

JavaScript redirects are not followed. Following one needs a real browser engine. When a page redirects with script, that is said plainly and no destination is claimed.

Some targets refuse automated requests. A 403 from a bot-protection service is reported as a refusal, not as a dead link: they are different facts. Changing “ask as” often changes the answer, which is itself worth knowing.

Nothing here judges whether a link is safe. It reports where the link goes. Deciding whether to visit it is yours.

Only ports 80 and 443 are dialled, and only publicly routable addresses. A redirect into a private network is refused and reported rather than followed.

Using this from the terminal 
curl '{{.Base}}/trace?u=http%3A%2F%2Fexample.com%2F&ua=googlebot'

ua takes the same names as the menu: browser, googlebot, bingbot, twitterbot, slackbot, facebook, gptbot, curl. Hops come back in order, each with its status, timing and notes, and final is absent when the chain did not end.
