How repeated keys are compared

Repeated keys are compared position by position within the key, never collapsed to last-wins. ?id=1&id=2 and ?id=2&id=1 are a real difference, and a comparison built on a map cannot see it at all.

Two URLs can differ as text and still describe the same request: different percent-encoding case, a default port written out, a resolved dot segment. When that is what happened it is said plainly rather than reported as a difference.
