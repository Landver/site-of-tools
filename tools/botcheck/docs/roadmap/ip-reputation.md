| G40 | Crowd-blending / fingerprint rarity / uniqueness entropy | CreepJS, Fingerprint.com, iphey.com, AmIUnique.org, EFF Cover Your Tracks, deviceandbrowserinfo.com, Pixelscan | low · ml-or-db · Deferred (documented, w/ concrete reason as of 2026-07-21) | Score fingerprint vs visitor population: rarity/'one in X', Shannon-entropy bits per attribute, crowd-blending score w/ letter grades, or outlier detection vs real-people fingerprint DB — rare/impossible fingerprint reads as fake. → **Deferred as *scoring* signal — 2026-07-21 gave deferral concrete reason not "not a priority": rarity does **not** discriminate bots at self-test tool's scale. Almost every visitor new, so exact fingerprint *maximally rare* (seen once) — describes first-time human & bespoke automation client identically. Rarity only separates the two vs large population where per-attribute frequencies known ("your GPU is 1-in-10 000"), & computing those requires storing per-attribute fingerprint detail per visitor (not current one-way full-fingerprint hash) — privacy cost not worth paying for signal that doesn't discriminate. A rarity/'one in X' *informational* readout (à la AmIUnique/EFF, explicitly not a verdict) remains possible future transparency feature, gated on same per-attribute storage decision.
