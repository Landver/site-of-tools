    What this checks

    The score comes from three things: your browser's own JavaScript fingerprint, your HTTP request headers, and the reputation of your IP address (datacenter, VPN, proxy, Tor). The strongest checks are the cross-checks.

    It does not use behavioral tracking, mouse or keystroke analysis, or machine learning. The one thing stored is a rolling 30-day fingerprint corpus to catch scraping farms. Sightings expire automatically.
